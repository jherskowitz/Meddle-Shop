About

Nosy. Maker. Noise Maker.

Hi, I'm J Herskowitz and Meddle Shop is where I stick my nose into making things where I really have no business doing so. It's an umbrella to house the various things I may be building — writing code, hacking hardware, woodworking, capturing photographs, composing music, and exploring whatever else sparks interest.

The philosophy is simple: try to be brave enough to suck at something new.
