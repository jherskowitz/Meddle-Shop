About

A one-man maker studio.

Meddle Shop is where curiosity meets craft. It's a solo studio dedicated to making things across disciplines — writing code, designing circuits, capturing photographs, composing music, and exploring whatever else sparks interest.

The philosophy is simple: stay curious, ship things, and never stop learning. Every project is an excuse to dig deeper into a craft and make something worth sharing.
